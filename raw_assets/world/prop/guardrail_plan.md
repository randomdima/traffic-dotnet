# Intercity road props

Roadside furniture for an intercity road (`RoadClass.Intercity`), which carries no sidewalk, car park or building.
Kept apart from plan.md so `qq townprops catalog` off that plan never puts these in the town's scatter: the engine
places them along an intercity road itself. A turned look's x is along the road; its bottom edge is the side facing the
traffic. A rail's ends are cut square so sections butt end to end: posts sit at a quarter of a 4 m section's length from
each end and at the middle of a 2 m one, which keeps them 2 m apart along any run.
