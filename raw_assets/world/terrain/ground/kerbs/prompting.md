# Prompting kerbs

What the kerbs' briefs taught. Read before writing the next one; the brief is in each frame's record
(`qq sprites trace kerb_concrete --full`).

**A kerb is a strip the ground repeats along every kerb line** (TER-3d): 6 × 0.2 m, its bottom edge the low side, where
the riser drops to the road or the grass. The kerbs were asked from a layout and squashed across onto it whatever height
they were drawn at (before 2026-10-25); asked in words, a strip is sized by its 0.2 m depth and is as long as it was
drawn. `use` cuts it at two of its own joints, the strongest near each end
(`at_joints`), keeping the stones between whole and writing the length one picture covers (`pictureM`).

## kerbs_a (2026-10-25) — five street kerbs and four footway edgings, one attempt, nine kept, $0.03

Kept from v1, all at 4: `kerb_concrete-v1` (used), `edging_concrete-v1` (used), `kerb_granite`, `kerb_concrete_worn`,
`kerb_granite_warm`, `kerb_bevelled`, `edging_setts`, `edging_concrete_moss`, `edging_charcoal`.

- **Worked at once:** the stone counts asked ("exactly 3 stones, joints at one third and two thirds"; 6; 15 setts),
  thin dark joints square across, square-cut ends that repeat, and the low side as "a darker band of the same stone
  along the bottom, about a quarter of its height … one thin light line where the top edge catches the light".
  Every material read as asked, the moss in the joints included.
- **Drawn 1.3 to 1.6 times the outline's height** however thin it is, every cell, centred on it: harmless for a
  strip laid at the kerb's width, so `cut` fits a kerb across at any scale.
- **Three stones a strip leave two once made to repeat**, and a strip that short repeats every two metres: kerbs_b
  asked for six-metre strips.

## kerbs_b (2026-10-25) — the same nine at 6 m, soft and varied, one attempt, nine kept, $0.03

The owner on kerbs_a in the game: "Dont do contrast black border separation. Make some variations, and 'real' feel".
Kept from v1: `kerb_granite-v2` (used), `edging_setts-v2` (used), `kerb_concrete_worn`, `kerb_granite_warm`,
`edging_concrete_moss`, `edging_charcoal` (4); `kerb_concrete`, `kerb_bevelled`, `edging_concrete` (3, their stones
uneven in length).

- **Worked:** "No black lines and no outlines anywhere. The joints … hairline gaps only a shade darker than the stone,
  sometimes filled with pale grit" and "No two stones alike … but the kerb as a whole one even colour from a distance"
  gave soft joints and stones of their own shade and wear, at "low contrast overall".
- **The joints are laid by eye**, a few per cent off even, and two plain concrete kerbs came back with five stones of
  two lengths: a strip cross-faded at one pitch ghosts two stones over each other, so a kerb is cut joint to joint.
- **"30 small setts" in a 20 cm strip came back as two rows of about 14** (`edging_setts`): it reads as small setts all
  the same, and was kept.
